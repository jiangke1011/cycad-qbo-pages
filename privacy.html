Privacy Policy

Cycad Management LLC Internal Accounting Integration Tool

This tool is an internal integration used solely by Cycad Management LLC and its affiliated entities to connect their own QuickBooks Online companies to their own internal accounting workflows. It is not a publicly distributed application.

What data this tool accesses: accounting data already present in the connected QuickBooks Online company, including the chart of accounts, vendor records, bills, and journal entries. This tool does not access, request, or store any personal data about individuals beyond what already exists within the connected company's own QuickBooks Online records (such as vendor contact names already on file).

How this data is used: solely to read existing accounts and vendors in order to correctly record new, previously reviewed and approved accounting transactions (bills and journal entries) into the connected company.

Data storage: connection credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored locally, only on infrastructure controlled by Cycad Management LLC, and are not transmitted to or shared with any third party.

Data sharing: this tool does not share, sell, or transmit any data to any third party. All data remains within the connected QuickBooks Online company and Cycad Management LLC's own internal systems.

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to Cycad Management LLC.
