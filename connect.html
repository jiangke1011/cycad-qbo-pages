Connecting this tool to QuickBooks Online

This is an internal accounting automation tool used only by Cycad Management LLC staff. Connecting (or reconnecting) a QuickBooks Online company to this tool is done directly by Cycad Management LLC staff running the tool locally -- there is no public sign-up or self-service connection flow on this page.

If you are Cycad Management LLC staff looking to connect or reconnect a company, run the tool's authorization step directly rather than through this page.
